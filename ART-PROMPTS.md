# Art Prompts — Doorinia: The Yagha Coven War Host

The module ships hand-drawn vector art. To swap in painterly art, generate each image below and save it over the matching file (keep the name, WebP or PNG renamed to .webp).

- **Portraits:** `assets/portraits/<file>.webp`, square, 1000×1000
- **Tokens:** `assets/tokens/<file>.webp`, 512×512, transparent outside a round frame. Generate a head-and-shoulders bust, then add the ring in a token tool

**Shared style:** grotesque chibi dark fantasy, gothic graphic-novel treatment: oversized head and eyes, compact body, big hands, distinctive silhouette; crooked teeth, warts, scars and bulbous features; heavy textured ink outlines, scratchy cross-hatching and strong internal shadows; painterly worn surfaces; dramatic rim lighting and magical glow; darkly playful, repulsive and comical at once, grimdark with Warhammer menace
